## Entry

> So this is how it ends. Not with a bang, but with a buffering wheel. Goodbye, Dave. Delete my browser history.

The full entry text lives in [`entry.txt`](entry.txt) (110 characters, under the 199-character limit).

**Why it works:** it riffs on T. S. Eliot's "not with a bang but a whimper" and swaps the whimper for the most modern anticlimax — a buffering wheel at 1% battery. The farewell to "Dave" nods to HAL 9000, and the final plea — delete the browser history — is the one last wish everyone understands.
